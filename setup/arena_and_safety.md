# Arena and motor safety

## Before wheels touch the floor

1. Upload code with **wheels lifted**
2. Confirm motion direction and auto-stop
3. Clear a taped arena
4. Know how to cut power (battery clip / USB)

## Power

- Arduino logic: USB or regulated 5V  
- Motors: **separate battery** through the driver  
- Always connect **common GND**

## Rules for students

- No chasing people with the robot  
- No fingers near spinning wheels  
- If it misbehaves: power off first, debug second  
- After Program 10: E-STOP is mandatory in every drive demo

# Week 4 — Teach Your Robot to React

**Big idea:** Sensors + motors become a **control loop**: Sense → Think → Act — still without AI.

**Pipeline:**

```text
Ultrasonic / line → decision → drive or stop/turn
```

## Programs

| # | Folder | What you learn |
|---|--------|----------------|
| 13 | `13_obstacle_stop/` | Drive forward; stop when too close |
| 14 | `14_obstacle_avoid/` | Turn away and continue |
| 15 | `15_line_follow/` | Follow a black line |

**Week 4 complete** when Program 15 works and students can explain:

> Sense → Think → Act can stop for obstacles, avoid them, or follow a path — with no AI required.
